Bet rows show the running leader after each completed hole (P1 +2 = Player 1 leads by two holes). A dash means that hole has not been scored. Nassau presses appear as additional rows once confirmed.

5 · Nassau presses

When a player goes two down, a new press can start on the following hole. Presses require confirmation; a pressed match can also be pressed again.

5 · Hammer controls

For each hole, the first thrower is selected once at the start of the match. After acceptance, the accepting player controls the Hammer and may throw it back. A declined Hammer ends that hole's bet; the thrower retains possession for the next hole.
